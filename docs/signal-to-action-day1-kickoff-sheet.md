# Signal-to-Action Day 1 Kickoff Sheet

Use this one page only for Day 1.

## Day 1 Objective

Create the initial signal pool for the 2-week pilot.

Target:
- Capture 10 to 15 candidate signals with timing and evidence.

## Files You Need

- `templates/signal-intake-template.md`
- `docs/pattern-ops-signal-source-map.md`
- `docs/signal-to-action-2-week-pilot-checklist.md`

## Day 1 Action Steps

1. Create 10 to 15 signal entries using `signal-intake-template`.
2. For each signal, fill:
   - Signal Name
   - Signal Domain
   - Signal Type
   - Trigger Window (start and end date)
   - Lead Time Needed
   - Why This Signal Matters
   - Evidence Links (minimum 2, target 3)
3. Assign preliminary Source Tier and Confidence.
4. Mark obvious weak signals as `Hold` in Notes (do not delete yet).

## Fast Capture Rules

- Prefer event/timing-specific signals over vague trends.
- Prefer signals with known dates.
- Use at least one source that can be verified quickly.
- If trigger timing is unclear, keep it but mark `Needs Validation`.

## Recommended Mix (for 10 to 15 signals)

- 3 Seasonal/Event
- 3 Market/Macro
- 2 Market Structure (IPO/index/reconstitution)
- 2 Content/Platform
- 1 Sports/Global Event
- 1 Wildcard (high-upside, lower confidence)

## Owner Split (Day 1)

- Atlas: signal collection and evidence links
- Kirin: framing Why This Signal Matters
- Strategos: keeps list balanced across domains
- Helios: final Day 1 close decision

## Day 1 Deliverables

1. 10 to 15 completed signal intake records
2. One short list:
   - Strong candidates
   - Needs validation
   - Hold

## Done Criteria (Day 1 Complete When True)

- At least 10 signals are documented.
- Every signal has a trigger window.
- Every signal has evidence links.
- Domain mix is balanced (not all one category).
- Helios marks Day 1 as complete.

## End-of-Day Handoff Prompt

Read my Day 1 signal intake records and run an Atlas validation pass using `docs/pattern-ops-signal-source-map.md`.  
Return:  
1) signals to keep,  
2) signals to hold,  
3) missing evidence per signal,  
4) top 6 signals to carry into Day 2.
