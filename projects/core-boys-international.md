# Core Boys International

## Project Purpose

Core Boys International is a short-form international fan-content brand built around streamer and creator clips.

The brand focuses on taking funny, chaotic, entertaining, or interesting creator moments and making them easier for international audiences to understand through subtitles, translations, edits, and platform-specific packaging.

## Example Template: Quick Funny Clip Translation

Use this for normal funny moments.

Video length:
12 to 30 seconds

Purpose:
Fast TikTok, Reels, and Shorts content.

Best for:
Jason jokes, Lacy one-liners, Ronaldo rage moments, Silky reactions.

Structure:

0:00 to 0:02:
Hook text

Examples:
- German fans need to understand this Jason moment 😭
- This Lacy clip is 10x funnier in French

0:02 to 0:25:
Main clip

Use the funniest part only. Cut dead air.

Throughout clip:
Dual subtitles

Top subtitle:
Original English

Bottom subtitle:
Translated language

Final 1 to 2 seconds:
Comment bait

Examples:
- Who should I translate next?
- What language should I do next?

Layout:
- Top hook text
- Center video
- Bottom subtitles
- Small corner logo watermark

Editing Notes:
- Use punch-ins for reactions
- Cut dead air
- Keep hooks simple
- Use emojis lightly
- Export for vertical 9:16 format
